# Experts 🧩

> **Projects end. Platforms don't.**

Clients invest in data platforms, analytics and AI solutions. Then the project team moves on, and the knowledge walks out the door. Experts is how we stop that: specialists who work inside the client's team, keep what was built running, and help the client's own people grow into the roles the organisation needs.

The goal isn't dependency on Plainsight. It's the client's own capability, with our support for as long as they need it.

On the website: [plainsight.pro/experts](https://www.plainsight.pro/experts).

## Who we place

| Role | What they take on |
|---|---|
| **Data engineers** | Pipelines running, platform healthy, data reliable. The infrastructure everything else depends on. |
| **Analytics engineers** | The bridge from raw data to business meaning: transformation logic, semantic models, the reports people rely on daily. |
| **AI engineers** | Production AI kept reliable and improving: models, integrations, monitoring, and new capability on top. |
| **Software engineers** | The custom applications and integrations the business runs on, kept healthy while new features ship. |
| **Project managers** | A roadmap turned into momentum: delivery on track across teams, scope and risk managed, the right people aligned. |

## What makes it different from staffing

| | What the client can expect from you |
|---|---|
| **A teammate, not a contractor** | You learn the business context, the processes and the goals. You flag issues, suggest improvements, and care about what happens next. |
| **Knowledge transfer built in** | You pair with their people and explain the reasoning behind decisions, so their team builds competence from day one, not from a handover document. |
| **The whole of Plainsight behind you** | This playbook, our accelerators, our AI tools, and the experience of 40+ colleagues. Ask in Teams before you solve something alone. |
| **Seniority that matches the title** | Trained, certified, and working within our methodology. |

If you've done the job well, the client's own team takes over in the end. That's the point.

## Fixed and flexible capacity

Most teams need a steady base plus a bit more when the quarter asks for it. The client commits to a fixed part and scales a flexible part up or down as the work moves. No minimum hours, and no paying for a full-time seat they only need half the time.
