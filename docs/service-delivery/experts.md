# Experts 🧩

> **Projects end. Platforms don't.**

Clients invest in data platforms, analytics and AI. Then the project team moves on, and the knowledge walks out the door with it. Experts is how we prevent that: specialists who join the client's team, keep what was built running and improving, and help the client's own people grow into it.

The goal isn't a client who depends on Plainsight. It's a client whose own team can carry it, with us next to them for as long as that helps.

On the website: [plainsight.pro/experts](https://www.plainsight.pro/experts).

## The expertise

Data engineering, analytics engineering, AI engineering, software engineering and project management. The same people, methods and [technical guidelines](../technical-guidelines/why-technical-guidelines.md) as our projects.

Most teams need a steady base, plus extra when the quarter asks for it. So the client agrees on a fixed part and scales a flexible part up or down as the work moves.

## How you work as an expert

You're part of the client's team. Learn their processes and goals well enough to spot a problem before it reaches your desk, and say so when you do. Pair with their people and explain the reasoning behind your choices, so their team gets stronger while you're there, not on the day you hand over a document.

You're never on your own, either. This playbook, our accelerators, the Plainsight Brain and the rest of the team are one Teams message away. Ask before you solve something alone.

If you've done the job well, the client's own team takes over in the end. That's the point.
