# Plainsight Tone of Voice

> **Source of truth:** the [Plainsight Brand Kit](https://github.com/PlainsightPro/brand-kit), page `voice.html`. This document mirrors it for search. Applies to every piece of Plainsight-branded writing: proposals, slides, LinkedIn posts, one-pagers, client emails, website copy.

---

## How We Sound

We are a Belgian data and AI consultancy. We talk like senior practitioners who respect the reader's time. Plain words, concrete claims, no hype. Human, warm, intelligent, with accessible, calm confidence.

## Three Principles

1. **Clear, not clever.** Short sentences. Plain English or plain Dutch. Trade the buzzword for the thing it describes.
2. **Concrete, not vague.** Numbers, timeframes, named technologies. "30% faster" beats "significantly faster".
3. **Confident, not loud.** No caps-shouting, no stacked exclamation marks. The work speaks; we describe it.

## We Say / We Don't Say

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| We build scalable data platforms and analytics that deliver real ROI. | We leverage cutting-edge synergies to unlock transformative data journeys. |
| Your platform and AI products, 30% faster, using our signature methodology. | Dramatically accelerate your enterprise's AI maturity at scale. |
| A team of data and AI experts passionate about turning complexity into outcomes. | Industry-leading visionaries reshaping the future of intelligence. |
| Data engineers, analytics engineers, AI engineers keeping your engine running at peak performance. | Best-in-class talent partners for your digital transformation journey. |

The pattern: direct claims with a concrete outcome, quantified and scoped, naming real roles and real work. If a sentence could appear in any consultancy's feed, rewrite it.

## What We Avoid

- Generic consultancy speak and buzzwords without substance
- Inflated claims and hype language
- "Thrilled to announce" style LinkedIn theater
- Bashing other consultancy firms; we show the difference by what we do
- Text that reads AI-generated

## LinkedIn & Social

Knowledge-sharing first: every post should teach something. Customer-angled, structured formatting, concrete examples from real situations. One point made well, not five made badly. Posts alternate between technology and leadership topics. Emphasize involving end users from the start when building AI systems. Prioritize practical production challenges (governance, maintenance, integration) over hype. Closing slides and closing lines are reflective, not promotional.

## Language

Dutch for the Belgian market, English for broader reach. Both follow the same three principles.
